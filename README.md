# Hook Taxonomy Rotation

Plans a week of hooks rotating the five stock openings — call-out, their own question, contrarian, number/list promise, first-person proof — one shape per day, logged.

A free plugin from KAIROS AI Systems — one focused tool that does one job well for a
small business: no suite to learn, no setup project, no account required beyond your
agent host.

## What it does

Proof openings carry the proof burden: the result must exist and be citable. The log makes hook shape a measurable variable, not a taste.

## How to use it

Add this plugin to a compatible agent host (Cursor, Grok Bot, or another
agent-plugins host), then ask in plain language, for example:

> Use the Hook Taxonomy Rotation to help me: plans a week of hooks rotating the five stock openings — call-out, their own question, contrarian, number/list promise, first-person proof — one shape per day, logged — I'll paste the details.

The plugin works from the facts you give it — your services, your prices, your
records. It never invents a number, a policy, or a result; anything it projects is
labelled an estimate with its basis. Drafts that would go to a customer stay drafts
until you approve them.
